Module 3 Building Units

Unit 1: Apply Basic Mathematics 1

Application of basic mathematical principles in construction and building activities.

Unit 2: Apply Communication Skills

Development and application of effective communication skills in the construction workplace.

Unit 3: Digital Literacy

Application of digital technologies and computer skills in learning and workplace activities.

Unit 4: Technical Drawing

Preparation, interpretation and application of technical drawings used in building construction.

Unit 5: Execute External Works

Performance of external construction works according to required specifications and procedures.

Unit 6: Doors and Windows

Preparation, installation and finishing of doors and windows in building construction.

Unit 7: Sub-Structure Works

Execution of construction activities associated with foundations and other sub-structure works.
