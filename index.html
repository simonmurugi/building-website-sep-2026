About Building & Plumbing

Developing practical skills, professionalism and industry-ready graduates.

Our Mission

To provide quality, practical and competency-based Building and Plumbing training that equips learners with relevant technical skills, professional values and the ability to respond effectively to industry and community needs.

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in Building and Plumbing technical training, producing innovative, competent and responsible professionals for the construction industry.

Training Highlights

Practical learning experiences designed to prepare students for real construction and plumbing work environments.

Building Construction

Hands-on training in construction practices, setting out, masonry, concrete work, finishing and other essential building skills.

CDACC practicals

Practical training in water supply systems, sanitary installations, pipework, fittings, maintenance and plumbing safety.

Site Practice

Students develop workplace skills through practical activities, teamwork, safety procedures and construction site practices.

Technical Skills Development

Emphasis is placed on accurate measurements, use of tools, problem solving, quality workmanship and professional standards.
